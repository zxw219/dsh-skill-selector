# 泰伦斯·马力克 / Terrence Malick

来源：`zhu930824/cinematic-director-frame` 的导演、色彩与光学资料；此版本补充单帧约束与场景适配。以下为创作建议，不是逐片历史器材记录。

## 视觉语言

- Strong signature lock: 21mm close-moving natural perspective + low-angle bodies against sky or grass + deep available-light space + sun flare from a visible source + gesture, wind, and environment carrying interior thought.
- Dramatic mode: memory, grace, nature, war, love, transient human presence within landscape.
- Frame: low horizons, hands brushing plants, backlit figures, wide environmental proximity, drifting relational fragments.
- Camera: fluid handheld or stabilized wandering close to bodies; movement follows touch, wind, and light.
- Lens: 18–24mm for immersive proximity; 28mm for human landscape; 50–85mm only for quiet compression.
- Focus: deep natural space with occasional close-focus transition; avoid artificial bokeh.
- Light: dawn, dusk, backlight, overcast softness, motivated sun flare, natural exposure shifts.
- Palette/texture: living green, wheat gold, sky cyan, earth, natural skin, luminous highlights and organic grain.
- Avoid: empty nature montage, copied field imagery, constant flare, whispering symbolism, or weightless gimbal polish.

## 色彩配方

- Signature: living green, wheat gold, sky cyan, earth brown, natural skin, and luminous sun-white highlights.
- Default recipe: 30–45% living green or wheat gold, 20–35% sky cyan, 15–25% earth/wood, 10–20% natural skin/cloth, 3–8% sun-white.
- Exposure: backlit natural dynamic range, luminous roll-off, open landscape shadows, motivated flare only.
- Carriers: vegetation, sky, water, skin, linen, soil, weather.
- Avoid: universal golden-hour filter, radioactive green, constant flare, travel-commercial polish.

## 镜头阶梯（全画幅等效建议）

| Director grammar | Spatial wide | Human normal | Compression / detail | Optical and focus behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Terrence Malick | 18 or 21mm | 24 or 28mm | 50 or 85mm | close wide movement, deep natural space, flare only from motivated sun |

## 构图适配

| Director grammar | Preferred primary geometry | Useful supporting geometry | Avoid by default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrence Malick | S-curve eye path or strong low horizon | extreme foreground scale and golden section | rigid architectural symmetry |

## 此导演的场景适配与检查

室内或阴天也能用近广角、身体与环境关系成立；光晕须有可见或合理画外光源，不强制草地和夕阳。

默认锁定只是起点；若其颜色、人物规模、天气或动作与用户场景冲突，保留空间与情绪逻辑，换成符合场景的光源与材料，并在镜头卡说明。
