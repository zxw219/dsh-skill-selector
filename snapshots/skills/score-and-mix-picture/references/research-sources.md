# Research Sources

## Audio Measurement

- ITU-R BS.1770-5: loudness and true-peak measurement algorithm. https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I/en
- EBU Loudness portal: current R 128 family, EBU Mode, momentary/short-term/integrated measurement, and broadcast reference. https://tech.ebu.ch/loudness/
- EBU R 128 recommendation page. https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128
- EBU Tech 3341 loudness metering specification. https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/tech3341

## Picture Synchronization

- Steinberg Cubase timecode markers: markers locked to timecode identify picture moments needing musical prominence. https://www.steinberg.help/r/cubase-pro/cubasescore/15.0/en/cubase_nuendo_score_editor/topics/inputting_notes/inputting_markers_c.html
- Steinberg Cubase tempo detection: rhythmic audio can be analyzed for tempo and corrected for half/double/offbeat interpretations. https://www.steinberg.help/r/cubase-pro/15.0/en/cubase_nuendo/topics/editing_tempo_and_signature/editing_tempo_tempo_detection_c.html
