## 核心基础风格包

> 可附加到任何场景描述后作为卢贝兹基基础风格锚定

### 标准版（适合大多数场景）
```
Emmanuel Lubezki cinematography, Chivo style, natural ambient light only,
no artificial fill light, wide angle lens (14-21mm), organic Steadicam movement,
ARRI Alexa digital with film-like texture, desaturated jewel tones,
naturalistic color grading, figure in environmental context, cinematic depth
```

### 精简版（字数限制场景）
```
Emmanuel Lubezki Chivo cinematography, natural light, ultra-wide angle,
fluid camera, desaturated organic colors, ARRI Alexa
```

### 强调自然光版
```
available light cinematography, no studio lighting, ambient natural light,
shadows preserved and embraced, magic hour warm light,
photojournalistic yet cinematic, Emmanuel Lubezki style
```

### 强调运镜版
```
Steadicam fluid movement, camera breathing with subject, orbiting continuous motion,
one-take aesthetic, immersive POV, camera alive and conscious, Lubezki movement style
```

## 按光线条件

### 黄金时段

```
magic hour cinematography, Lubezki golden hour style,
last 20 minutes before sunset warm orange-gold light,
long shadows extending across landscape, warm backlight creating rim light on figures,
sky transitioning from gold to blue, the light dying and precious,
shooting against fading light, every second different color temperature,
natural silhouette potential, amber warmth against cooling air

附加情绪标注: [romantic / melancholic / triumphant / desperate — 根据场景选择]
```

### 阴天漫射光

```
overcast diffused natural light, Lubezki cloud-filtered cinematography,
even shadowless illumination, colors revealed without directional contrast,
skin tones honest and unideal, no harsh specular highlights,
soft ambient wrap-around light, peaceful or desolate depending on context,
Malick Tree of Life morning light quality, no fill needed no shadows to fill

适合情绪: memory, childhood, meditation, transition, the ordinary made sacred
```

### 夜间火光

```
practical fire light only, Lubezki campfire night cinematography,
amber-orange flickering light source, faces half in warm light half in cold shadow,
background completely dark, no electric fill, absolute firelight primitivism,
organic light movement creating living image, high ISO grain embraced,
The Revenant campfire night quality

适合情绪: survival, storytelling, ritual, isolation, warmth against cold
```

### 冬日冷光

```
winter cold light cinematography, Lubezki The Revenant blue-grey light,
low sun angle creating long cold shadows, ice and snow as natural reflectors,
color temperature 7000-8000K blue-white daylight, desaturated winter palette,
breath visible in frozen air, natural light bouncing off snow as ambient fill,
stark contrast between warm subject and cold environment

适合情绪: survival, endurance, isolation, death and life in balance
```

## 按情绪类型

### 存在主义孤独

```
existential isolation cinematography, Lubezki cosmic humanist style,
solitary human figure against vast natural or cosmic environment,
ultra-wide angle making figure tiny, negative space dominant,
muted natural color palette, no emotional manipulation through artificial light,
environment indifferent to human presence, silence made visual,
figure as question mark in the universe

影片参照: The Revenant, Gravity, Knight of Cups
```

### 神性与超验

```
transcendent cinematography Lubezki Malick style, upward gaze toward light,
looking through tree canopy at sun with bokeh flares,
divine light quality — light as spiritual presence,
figures silhouetted against brilliant sky or window,
white diffused light suggesting infinity, tree of life visual language,
consciousness reaching beyond physical body, prayer and light

影片参照: The Tree of Life, The New World, Knight of Cups
```

### 混乱与生存

```
survival chaos cinematography, Lubezki immersive action style,
ultra-wide angle in midst of danger, Steadicam within the action not observing it,
subjective immersive perspective, camera as participant not spectator,
shaky urgency with organic Steadicam, natural light in hostile environment,
no safe distance, intimate crisis perspective

影片参照: The Revenant bear attack, Children of Men car chase
```

### 记忆与童年

```
childhood memory cinematography, Lubezki consciousness stream style,
warm amber diffused summer light, wide angle at child's eye level,
dreamy unfocused edges, emotional non-linear visual logic,
ordinary moments as sacred — grass, light, water, hands,
time perceived differently — slower or fragmented,
adults seen from below as giants, world enormous and mysterious

影片参照: The Tree of Life, Y Tu Mamá También
```

### DALL-E / GPT Image 格式

> 用自然语言描述，避免技术术语堆砌

```
[场景描述]

Shot in the style of Emmanuel Lubezki's cinematography:
- Only natural light sources — [具体说明光源]
- Ultra-wide angle perspective, creating a sense of [intimacy/scale/immersion]
- The camera feels alive, as if it's breathing with the subject
- Colors are naturalistic and slightly desaturated, like real life
- Deep shadows are preserved without fill light
- [情感意图描述]
```

**示例**：
```
A man standing at the edge of a frozen river at sunset

Shot in the style of Emmanuel Lubezki's cinematography:
- Only natural light — the last golden rays of sunset hitting from the left
- Ultra-wide angle perspective, making the man tiny against the vast frozen landscape
- The camera feels alive, hovering slightly, as if it too is cold
- Colors are naturalistic: ice-blue shadows, amber gold where the sun hits
- Deep shadows in the trees behind him are preserved without any fill
- A feeling of solitary survival, man against the indifferent natural world
```
