# Darius Khondji 风格 AI 提示词库

按场景类型与情绪分类的完整提示词模板。所有提示词以英文为主，可直接用于 Midjourney、Stable Diffusion、FLUX、DALL-E、Nano Banana Pro、ZenStudio。

每个模板分为 **Style（风格）** 与 **Scene（画面）**，可独立替换组合。

## 提示词架构

一个完整的 Khondji 风格提示词由 6 层构成：

```
[摄影师签名] + [工艺/胶片] + [打光方案] + [色彩调性] + [氛围/物质感] + [构图/场景]
```

### 基础签名层（所有提示词的前缀）

```
shot in the style of Darius Khondji, anamorphic 2.39:1,
bleach bypass silver retention process, single motivated light source,
volumetric atmospheric haze, 35mm film grain
```

```
Darius Khondji cinematography, bleach bypass, anamorphic, fog and neon
```

### 模型适配微调

**Midjourney 8.2：**
- 加 `--raw --ar 239:100 --stylize 250-400`
- 关键词置首：`bleach bypass, anamorphic, Khondji`

**FLUX / Stable Diffusion：**
- 首段堆叠核心词：`Darius Khondji cinematography, bleach bypass, silver retention process, anamorphic`
- 用负面提示词主动排除数字感

**Nano Banana Pro / ZenStudio：**
- 可中英混合，但工艺术语保留英文
- 强调 `Khondji style, bleach bypass, fog, single source`

**DALL-E / GPT Image：**
- 用描述句而非关键词：
  "Captured in the style of Darius Khondji, this image uses bleach bypass film processing with silver retention, resulting in deep crushed blacks, desaturated colors, and sickly cyan-green shadows with sodium yellow highlights. The scene is lit by a single practical light source, with heavy volumetric fog catching the light beam. Shot on 35mm film with anamorphic lens creating elliptical bokeh."
