## 十二、避免的反模式

以下关键词 / 风格会完全破坏威利斯美学，应避免：

| 反模式 | 为什么避免 | 替代 |
|--------|----------|------|
| `beautiful even lighting` | 威利斯拒绝均匀照明 | `uneven single-source` |
| `bright eye catch light` | 威利斯刻意让眼睛陷入阴影 | `eyes in brow shadow` |
| `fill light softening shadows` | 威利斯不补光 | `no fill, crushed shadows` |
| `rim light hair separation` | 威利斯不打轮廓光 | `silhouette against backlight` |
| `lens flare sun burst` | 威利斯几乎不让炫光 | `controlled practical source` |
| `vibrant saturated colors` | 威利斯色彩极克制 | `suppressed desaturated palette` |
| `anamorphic cinematic 2.39` | 威利斯不用变形镜 | `spherical 1.85 or 2.35` |
| `HDR bright shadows visible` | 威利斯要 crushed blacks | `deep underexposed blacks` |
| `modern digital clean 4K` | 威利斯是胶片美学 | `Fujifilm grain 1970s film` |
| `three-point portrait lighting` | 威利斯单光源 | `single top key only` |

## 十四、尾声

威利斯的美学本质是**减法**。当 AI 模型倾向于"多照亮、多细节、多颜色"时，我们必须用提示词反复强调：

- **少光** — single source, only one lamp
- **少细节** — crushed blacks, 70% of frame in shadow
- **少颜色** — suppressed palette, sepia tobacco, desaturated
- **少运动** — static composition, locked-off camera
- **少炫技** — no lens flare, no anamorphic, no HDR

记住威利斯的核心原则：
> **"It's not what you light — it's what you don't light."**

然后让 AI 把"不照亮的部分"画出来。
