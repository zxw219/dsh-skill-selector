# 赛尔乔·莱昂内 / Sergio Leone

来源：`zhu930824/cinematic-director-frame` 的导演、色彩与光学资料；此版本补充单帧约束与场景适配。以下为创作建议，不是逐片历史器材记录。

## 视觉语言

- Strong signature lock: choose either a 24mm landscape frame or a 180mm facial-detail frame + long stillness before decisive motion + low horizon + hard sun and dust + tactile eyes, hands, leather, and metal.
- Dramatic mode: waiting, ritualized confrontation, greed, mythic scale, time stretched before action.
- Frame: extreme wides, extreme close details, strong horizon, triangular confrontation, empty lateral distance.
- Camera: monumental stillness, slow push, or precise lateral reveal.
- Lens: 24–28mm for landscape scale; 50mm for body ritual; 135–180mm for eyes, hands, and compressed confrontation.
- Focus: deep landscape or razor-specific telephoto detail; avoid generic portrait blur.
- Light: hard directional sun, dusty backlight, dense shade, tungsten night practicals.
- Palette/texture: sun-baked ochre, tobacco brown, faded blue, iron black, sweat, dust, worn leather and wood.
- Avoid: copying western costumes, gun belts, iconic standoffs, or using extreme close-ups without causal tension.

## 色彩配方

- Signature: sun-baked ochre and tobacco brown against faded blue, iron black, and a small dusty red accent.
- Default recipe: 40–55% ochre/sand, 20–30% tobacco brown, 10–20% faded sky blue, 5–10% iron black, 3–6% dusty red.
- Exposure: hard sun, dense shade, hot highlights, dust-visible backlight, tactile skin without glamour.
- Carriers: earth, sky, worn cloth, timber, oxidized metal, sweat, dust.
- Avoid: orange wash, clean costume color, teal-orange western advertising.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sergio Leone | 24 or 28mm | 50mm | 135 or 180mm | extreme wide/tele contrast, deep landscape or compressed facial detail |

## 此导演的场景适配与检查

单帧必须选择广角环境或长焦细节其中一个；原锁定中的24mm与180mm是不同镜头的对比逻辑，不是单帧双焦段。

默认锁定只是起点；若其颜色、人物规模、天气或动作与用户场景冲突，保留空间与情绪逻辑，换成符合场景的光源与材料，并在镜头卡说明。

本地示例：[参考图](../assets/reference-frame.png)。原项目测试场景：Post-festival square。主要基准：extreme wide stillness, low horizon, ochre dust and red-object tension。
