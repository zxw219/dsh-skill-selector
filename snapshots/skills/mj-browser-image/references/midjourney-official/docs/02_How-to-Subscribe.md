# How to Subscribe

> 来源：https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/31974654274573
> 抓取时间：2026-11-05

---

To get started with a Midjourney plan, first create or log in to your account and head over to your [Manage Subscription Page](https://www.midjourney.com/account) on midjourney.com.

If you need help getting logged in for the first time or want to access an old account, don’t worry! Check out our [Logging In & Connecting Accounts](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33390994570509) article for step-by-step help.

## Pick a Plan

Once you're on your Manage Subscription page, scroll down to the plans section. Find your perfect plan and press the **Subscribe** button below it. If you're not sure which plan to choose, our [Comparing Midjourney Plans](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333) article can help you decide.

Note: All Midjourney plans are subscriptions, meaning they automatically renew on a monthly or yearly basis unless you set your plan to cancel.

> Yearly billing is selected by default, meaning the full year is paid upfront. If you'd rather pay each month, please click the "Monthly Billing" tab before selecting a plan.

## Complete the Checkout Process

To complete your purchase, check out using our payment processor, Stripe. If you have any questions during this process, we've got you covered with the helpful articles below.

Keep in mind that once your billing email is set during checkout, you'll need to reach out to our billing support team if you want to [change your email](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27868854770573). Remember, we [can't change invoices](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27868825749517) after they've been issued. So, double-check that the payment details you enter at checkout are exactly what you want to see on your invoice.

## Confirmation

After your payment goes through, you'll get a confirmation email. This email includes both your invoice and receipt. It will be sent to the billing email you used during the Stripe checkout process, which might be different from the one you use to log in to midjourney.com.

If you return to your Manage Subscription page, you should now see that your plan is active and ready to use.
