# Managing Your Midjourney Magazine Subscription

> 来源：https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870410169997
> 抓取时间：2026-11-05

---

Your Midjourney Magazine subscription is entirely separate from your image generation subscription.

To manage your magazine subscription, enter your magazine billing email into [this form](https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/aEUdRA5Wc8t25a03cc). This is the only method for managing your magazine subscription.

Important: Any changes you make to your image generation subscription on the Manage Subscription page at midjourney.com will not affect your magazine subscription. The two subscriptions operate independently.

After entering your email in the above form, you'll receive an email with a link to your magazine billing portal.

If you encounter any issues—such as the link directing you to your Midjourney image generation subscription instead of your magazine subscription—please [contact billing support](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32638309968141) for assistance.
