If you have any questions or need assistance, our billing support team is here to help.

#### How to Reach Billing Support

For billing inquiries, please email [billing@midjourney.com](mailto:billing@midjourney.com). Within a few minutes you'll receive an automated response that includes your support ticket number, and the support team will respond to your request as soon as possible.

#### Include Your Midjourney ID

To help us assist you faster, please include your Midjourney ID in your email. Your Midjourney ID lets us easily locate your account information and speed up the process.
