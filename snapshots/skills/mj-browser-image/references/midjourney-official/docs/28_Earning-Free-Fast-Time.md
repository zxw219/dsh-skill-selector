# Earning Free Fast Time

> 来源：https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/28014817524109
> 抓取时间：2026-11-05

---

Looking to add more Fast time to your account? Midjourney provides a few ways to earn extra [Fast time](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32016412137741), perfect for keeping your creative projects going! Just remember, you'll need an active subscription to use any awarded time, and it expires 30 days after it's given. You can also [purchase additional Fast time](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33570952624141) on our website.

#### Rating Images

Each day, the top 2,000 members who rate the most images receive an extra hour of Fast time free. If you're among the top raters, you’ll receive a direct message from the Midjourney Bot in Discord around midnight UTC. Curious about how to rate images? Check out our [Complete Tasks](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33390759197197) article for more details.

#### Surveys

Occasionally, we conduct surveys and offer Fast time as a reward. The amount you can earn varies, so keep an eye on the [Tasks page](https://www.midjourney.com/tasks) to find open surveys and see how much Fast time you can earn.
