## 六、负面提示词建议（Negative Prompts）

为避免现代化、过度风格化的失败结果，建议使用以下负面提示词：

```
modern digital sharpness, drone aerial perspective,
shallow depth of field bokeh blur, anamorphic lens flares,
oversaturated HDR look, contemporary Steadicam handheld,
cluttered foreground details, modern color grading teal-orange,
LUT-heavy crushed shadows, tilted Dutch angles,
fast cutting montage feel, vlog style framing,
shallow telephoto bokeh, smartphone wide angle distortion,
plastic CGI textures, 21st century clothing, clean modern surfaces
```

## 七、模型适配技巧

### Midjourney 8.2
```
[full prompt] --ar 7:3 --raw --stylize 100 --v 8.2
```
- 加 `--ar 7:3` 或 `--ar 11:5` 模拟 Super Panavision 70
- 加 `--raw` 获得胶片质感而非数字光泽
- 加 `--stylize 100` 保持中等风格化（不过度 MJ 化）

### Stable Diffusion / FLUX
- 推荐 LoRA：`vintage film stock`, `70mm cinematic`, `1960s color grading`
- 关键正面词：`shot on 65mm film, Eastmancolor, Freddie Young, David Lean epic, vast horizon`
- 关键负面词：见上方负面提示词

### DALL-E 3 / GPT Image
- 用自然语言："A vast desert with a tiny silhouette of a rider on the distant horizon, shot in the style of Freddie Young's Lawrence of Arabia, 65mm large format film, ultra-wide 2.20:1 aspect ratio, golden hour, no fill light"
- 强调 "patient natural light", "shot like a David Lean epic from 1962"

### Nano Banana Pro / ZenStudio (Gemini 3 Pro Image)
- 中英混合有效，但核心摄影术语用英文
- 建议结构：`[中文场景描述] + Freddie Young 65mm Super Panavision style, vast horizon, Lawrence of Arabia atmosphere`
- 例如："沙漠中骑骆驼的剪影队伍，Freddie Young Lawrence of Arabia style, 65mm Super Panavision 70, golden hour silhouette riders against burning sunset sky, 2.20:1 ultra-wide"

### Kling / Runway / Sora 视频
- 强调运镜："patient locked-off camera holding for full duration"（静止机位）
- 或："slow helicopter aerial sweeping across landscape"（直升机大全景）
- 避免现代术语 "drone aerial"，改用 "helicopter aerial 1960s style"
- 时长：建议 8 秒以上，让 Young 的耐心节奏有空间展开

**记住 Freddie Young 的核心箴言**：

> "You have to wait for the light. You can't make it. You have to be patient."

每一条提示词都应该体现这种"等待"的精神——光线、地平线、时刻、人物，都需要等到那个对的瞬间。这是 Young 与所有当代摄影师最大的区别，也是史诗摄影最神圣的纪律。
