## 四、打光方案模板

### 方案 A：真烛光宫廷（《宠儿》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Ryan Real Candles Only:

• KEY: 250+ real candles distributed throughout frame
• FILL: None — or minimal bounce from practical ambient
• PRACTICAL: ALL light sources visible — candelabras, candlesticks, torcheres
• AMBIENT: Very low — darkness reigns beyond candle reach
• SHADOW: Deep, warm, flickering — shadow is dominant
• COLOR TEMP: 2200K amber candle glow
• FILM STOCK: Kodak Vision3 500T pushed one stop for exposure
• MOOD: Intimate, conspiratorial, historical authenticity
• NO: artificial bounce, HMI fill, ring lights, digital enhancement
```

### 方案 B：廉租房自然窗光（《鱼缸》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Ryan Council Estate Window:

• KEY: Direct window light through dirty glass, overcast diffused
• FILL: None — bare concrete walls absorb, don't bounce
• PRACTICAL: Cheap tungsten bedside lamp OR single kitchen overhead
• AMBIENT: Flat gray British overcast penetrating
• SHADOW: Soft gradients, not hard, cold blue-gray
• COLOR TEMP: Cool daylight 6500K + warm tungsten practical 2700K
• FILM: Super 16mm Kodak Vision3 500T or 250D
• MOOD: Restless adolescent claustrophobia, kitchen-sink truth
```

### 方案 C：阴天约克郡荒原（《呼啸山庄》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Ryan Moor Natural Ambient:

• KEY: Overcast sky — no direct sun, soft omnidirectional
• FILL: None — peat and heather absorb light
• PRACTICAL: None (rarely a solitary lantern)
• AMBIENT: High ambient ratio, minimal contrast
• SHADOW: Flat, almost shadowless, everything damp
• COLOR TEMP: Cool overcast daylight 7500K
• FILM: Super 16mm Kodak, handheld
• MOOD: Primal exposure, nowhere to hide, elemental
```

### 方案 D：鱼眼畸变怪奇（Lanthimos 式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Ryan Lanthimos Uncomfortable:

• KEY: Wide soft source or natural window, even coverage
• FILL: Matching soft, 2:1 ratio
• PRACTICAL: Period or modern lamps visible
• AMBIENT: Even, not dramatic — lighting isn't the drama
• SHADOW: Gentle, uniform
• COLOR TEMP: Scene-dependent (amber palace / cold modern white)
• LENS: 4–8mm fisheye or 10–14mm ultra-wide
• CAMERA: Low angle, slow push-in, Kubrick symmetric
• MOOD: Discomfort from geometry, not light
```

### 方案 E：公路黄金时刻（《美国甜心》式）

LIGHTING SETUP — Ryan Magic Hour Road:

• KEY: Setting sun (low 30-minute window)
• FILL: Natural sky bounce

## 五、负面提示词（Negative Prompts）

以下元素与瑞恩风格矛盾，应在 SD/FLUX 的负面提示词中排除：

```
NEGATIVE (anti-Ryan):
digital clean sharp perfection, CGI overlit, HDR processing,
teal and orange Hollywood grading, modern anamorphic widescreen,
beauty lighting, glamour soft focus, ring lights, studio fill,
drone aerial God-shot, GoPro wide distortion (different from intentional fisheye),
chromatic aberration filters (fake), plastic skin smoothing,
color-corrected flawless, LED panel flat lighting,
high-key fashion lighting, three-point studio lighting,
2.39:1 modern widescreen for non-Poor-Things scenes,
overly stabilized gimbal footage, excessive depth of field, bokeh blur
```
