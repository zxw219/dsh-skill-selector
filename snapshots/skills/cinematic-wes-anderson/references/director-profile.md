# 韦斯·安德森 / Wes Anderson

来源：`zhu930824/cinematic-director-frame` 的导演、色彩与光学资料；此版本补充单帧约束与场景适配。以下为创作建议，不是逐片历史器材记录。

## 视觉语言

- Strong signature lock: 32mm frontal symmetry + flat lateral blocking + moderate-deep focus + even designed light + strict limited palette and deadpan object choreography, without copying signature IP.
- Dramatic mode: emotional distance expressed through theatrical order, collections, rituals, and deadpan behavior.
- Frame: frontal symmetry, lateral profile staging, flat architectural layers, centered objects.
- Camera: locked frontal view, precise lateral tracking, or axial move.
- Lens: default 27mm, 32mm, or 40mm depending subject scale.
- Focus: moderate-to-deep, keeping the designed world legible.
- Light: even storybook daylight or clean motivated interiors.
- Palette/texture: controlled limited palette, tactile miniatures/sets, period materials, restrained graphic color blocks.
- Avoid: copying a known hotel, uniform, typography system, or character; avoid turning every frame into cute whimsy.

## 色彩配方

- Signature: large tactile pastel color blocks, complementary but dusty hue pairs, warm cream structure, and one darker grounding color.
- Default recipe: 30–40% dusty mint or powder blue, 20–30% warm cream, 12–20% mustard, 8–15% brick red or faded coral, 10–20% dark brown/charcoal grounding.
- Alternate recipe: dusty rose + pale teal + burgundy + cream. Choose one recipe only.
- Exposure: even designed light, clean midtones, restrained contrast, clearly separated color blocks.
- Carriers: walls, benches, doors, wardrobe, luggage, upholstery, floor tile, practical fixtures.
- Avoid: random candy colors, low-saturation beige wash, copying a known hotel palette, cute color without emotional function.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wes Anderson | 24 or 27mm | 32 or 40mm | 75mm | rectilinear frontal geometry, moderate-deep focus, minimal breathing |

## 构图适配

| Director grammar | Preferred primary geometry | Useful supporting geometry | Avoid by default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wes Anderson | bilateral symmetry and frontal grid | lateral repetition | accidental asymmetry, generic thirds |

## 此导演的场景适配与检查

秩序与人物的细微失序形成情绪；避免将所有场景变成粉彩童话，按年代与材质选择色块。

默认锁定只是起点；若其颜色、人物规模、天气或动作与用户场景冲突，保留空间与情绪逻辑，换成符合场景的光源与材料，并在镜头卡说明。
