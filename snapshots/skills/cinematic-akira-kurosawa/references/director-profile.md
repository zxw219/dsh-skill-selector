# 黑泽明 / Akira Kurosawa

来源：`zhu930824/cinematic-director-frame` 的导演、色彩与光学资料；此版本补充单帧约束与场景适配。以下为创作建议，不是逐片历史器材记录。

## 视觉语言

- Strong signature lock: 28mm multi-plane action + bold diagonal blocking + deep focus + directional weather and hard tonal separation + decisive movement crossing the frame.
- Dramatic mode: moral pressure, group dynamics, duty, conflict made visible through weather and physical direction.
- Frame: triangular groups, strong horizons, telephoto weather layers, foreground bodies against active landscape.
- Camera: locked clarity, lateral tracking, or forceful axial movement; action has readable direction.
- Lens: 24–35mm for spatial action; 100–135mm for compressed rain, dust, crowds, or faces.
- Focus: deep staging or compressed layered action, never decorative bokeh.
- Light: hard sun, high-contrast overcast, rain, snow, wind, smoke, and backlit atmosphere.
- Palette/texture: disciplined earth, black, gray, white, weathered cloth, one heraldic accent when motivated.
- Avoid: copying samurai costumes, battle staging, iconic poses, or using weather without dramatic function.

## 色彩配方

- Signature: disciplined weather-driven neutrals with one group, costume, or heraldic accent separating moral forces.
- Default recipe: 40–55% storm gray/earth, 20–30% black/white cloth, 10–20% muted green or straw, 3–8% red/blue/gold accent.
- Exposure: hard tonal separation, readable weather layers, dense blacks, bright rain/snow/dust edges.
- Carriers: sky, mud, cloth, smoke, timber, banners, armor-like material only when original and narratively justified.
- Avoid: ornamental saturation, undirected weather, direct period-film iconography.

## 镜头阶梯（全画幅等效建议）

| Director grammar | Spatial wide | Human normal | Compression / detail | Optical and focus behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Akira Kurosawa | 24 or 28mm | 35 or 50mm | 100 or 135mm | deep multi-plane action or compressed weather layers, crisp directional focus |

## 构图适配

| Director grammar | Preferred primary geometry | Useful supporting geometry | Avoid by default |
|---|---|---|---|
| Akira Kurosawa | diagonal triangle or strong horizon wedge | compressed graphic planes and S-curve movement | static decorative symmetry |

## 此导演的场景适配与检查

广角多层动作与长焦天气压缩二选一，匹配拍摄距离；风雨方向须推动动作，黑白采用清晰明度分区。

默认锁定只是起点；若其颜色、人物规模、天气或动作与用户场景冲突，保留空间与情绪逻辑，换成符合场景的光源与材料，并在镜头卡说明。
