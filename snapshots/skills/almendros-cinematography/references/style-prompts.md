## 八、阿尔门德罗斯绝对禁忌（提示词永远不要写的词）

- ❌ `studio lighting` / `three-point lighting`
- ❌ `dramatic side light from offscreen rig`
- ❌ `lens flare` / `anamorphic flares`
- ❌ `bright fill light` / `key light fill`
- ❌ `HDR` / `hyper-realistic`
- ❌ `cool fluorescent skin` / `pale skin`
- ❌ `oversaturated colors` / `vivid neon`
- ❌ `shaky cam` / `handheld documentary chaos`（除了部分 Truffaut 段落）
- ❌ `Dutch angle` / `extreme low angle`
- ❌ `cinematic lighting`（太空泛，要写具体光源）
