# Gregg Toland 风格 AI 提示词库

按场景类型、情绪和影片参照分类的完整提示词模板，可直接用于 AI 图像/视频生成。

**重要前提**：托兰德 95% 作品为黑白——本提示词库默认 `black and white`，除非明确标注彩色。

## 提示词架构

一个完整的托兰德风格提示词由 6 层构成：

```
[摄影师签名] + [设备模拟] + [打光方案] + [黑白调性] + [深焦构图] + [场景/氛围]
```


## 四、打光方案模板

### 方案 A：单油灯（最经典 1940s 托兰德）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Toland Single Oil Lamp:

• KEY: Single hanging oil lamp or table lamp (visible in frame), warm tungsten ~2500K
• FILL: None — deep shadow on opposite side of room
• PRACTICAL: The lamp itself is the only light source
• HIDDEN: Small Mole-Richardson Baby (200W Fresnel) hidden behind furniture
        to extend lamp illumination realistically
• AMBIENT: Very low — most of room in deep darkness
• SHADOW: Hard, directional, falling on walls and faces
• CEILING: Often visible (low angle) — no top light possible
• MOOD: Intimate, period-authentic, naturalistic, slightly oppressive
• B&W ZONE: Zone II–III shadows, Zone V skin, Zone VIII–IX lamp itself
```

### 方案 B：低角度天花板（《公民凯恩》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Toland Low Angle Ceiling Shot:

• KEY: Hidden Mole-Richardson Baby/Junior Fresnel from camera-side at floor level,
       angled upward 30-40° to simulate room ambient
• FILL: None or very minimal bounce from floor
• PRACTICAL: Lamp visible in frame (table lamp or wall sconce)
• CEILING: REAL ceiling built (often muslin or wood beams) — no top lighting
• AMBIENT: Low — letting ceiling go dark adds weight
• SHADOW: Hard upward-cast shadows on ceiling and upper walls
• CAMERA: Mitchell BNC dug into floor pit or extreme low platform
• MOOD: Oppressive, monumental, characters towering, power dynamics inverted
• B&W ZONE: Ceiling Zone II, walls Zone IV–V, faces Zone VI, highlights Zone VIII
```

### 方案 C：表现主义硬光阴影（《Mad Love》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Toland Expressionist Shadow:

• KEY: Single Mole-Richardson 2KW Fresnel as hard directional source,
       angled at 45° or higher for dramatic shadow casting
• FILL: NONE — embracing extreme contrast
• SHADOW PROPS: Cast objects (railing, blinds, characters) between key and wall
       to throw geometric shadow patterns
• PRACTICAL: None or single decorative
• AMBIENT: Black — Zone 0 areas dominate
• MOOD: Psychological menace, gothic, German expressionism reborn
• B&W ZONE: Zone 0 deep blacks dominate, Zone X bright highlights only on key surface
• REFERENCE: Murnau, Lang, but in American narrative context
```

### 方案 D：深焦多层（《公民凯恩》《Best Years》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Toland Deep Focus Multi-Plane:

• APERTURE: f/8 to f/16 (small aperture for max depth of field)
• REQUIRES: High-speed Eastman Super-XX film + Opticoat coated lenses
• KEY (foreground plane): Hidden Baby spotlight, motivated by visible lamp
• KEY (middle plane): Second Baby spotlight, possibly motivated by window
• KEY (background plane): Third light source, often motivated by another window/lamp
• FILL: Minimal — only enough to retain shadow detail at each depth
• AMBIENT: Each plane lit independently to register exposure at small aperture
• PRACTICAL: Visible lamps/windows at multiple depths providing logical motivation
• MOOD: Democratic visual access, three simultaneous dramas
• B&W ZONE: Each plane balanced to occupy Zone V skin tones, full range across frame
• CAMERA: 25mm Bausch & Lomb at f/16, focused at hyperfocal distance
```

### 方案 E：荒野自然光（《呼啸山庄》《Grapes of Wrath》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Toland Natural Exterior:

• KEY: Sun (preferably hard direct sunlight)
• FILL: Sky / ground bounce only — no artificial fill
• REFLECTORS: Rare — toland preferred natural look
• ATMOSPHERIC: Smoke or dust particles to give air visible texture
• TIMING: Often shot in magic hour or under partial cloud cover for ideal contrast
• MOOD: Naturalist, weathered, documentary-poetic
• B&W ZONE: Sky often Zone VII–VIII, landscape Zone IV–V, shadow valleys Zone II
• 25mm wide angle to include sweeping horizon
```

### 方案 F：海上夜雾（《Long Voyage Home》式）

```
LIGHTING SETUP — Toland Maritime Night Fog:

• KEY: Single swinging oil lamp on rope (motion adds atmosphere)
• ATMOSPHERIC: Heavy smoke/fog machine fills entire set
• MOON: Large 5KW arc lamp through cyclorama backing as moonlight
• PRACTICAL: Lanterns held by sailors, additional point sources
• FILL: NONE — fog provides natural ambient diffusion
• SHADOW: Soft from fog scattering, but hard near direct lamp
• MOOD: Melancholic, isolated, maritime poetry
• B&W ZONE: Zone IV–VI dominant gray fog, Zone IX lamp source, Zone I deep sea
• CAMERA: 25mm to capture fog volumetrics
```

## 六、画幅比例推荐

| 比例 | 用途 | 托兰德实例 |
|------|------|-----------|
| **1.37:1（学院比例）** | 1932–1953 标准——**所有黑白时期托兰德作品** | 《公民凯恩》《呼啸山庄》《黄金时代》《长别离》全部 |
| **1.33:1（早期）** | 1929–1932 早期作品 | 《Bulldog Drummond》《The Devil to Pay!》 |
| **1.85:1** | 不适用——托兰德时代尚未出现 | — |
| **2.39:1** | 不适用——CinemaScope 1953 才出现，托兰德 1948 已逝 | — |

**重要**：托兰德作品**永远不要**用宽银幕比例（2.39:1 / 1.85:1）。学院比例（1.37:1）是历史正确选择。

**Midjourney 适配**：
- `--ar 4:3` 或 `--ar 137:100` → 学院比例（最准确）
- `--ar 1:1` → 不推荐
- 永远不要 `--ar 16:9` 或 `--ar 7:3`

```
luminous highlights with fine grain structure, full Zone 0 to Zone X tonal range,

high-density blacks with detail retention, archival film aesthetic
```
